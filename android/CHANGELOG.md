# Changelog: The Daemon's Apprentice for Android

Releases are tagged `android-vX.Y`. The desktop game has its own
changelog in the repository's root.

## 1.0

The first version: the terminal game on Android phones and tablets,
played in the phone's own shell.

- **Levels 0 to 15.** The basics on the phone (Hello, Terminal to Building
  Blocks), then six levels about Android itself: SELinux (The Wall You
  Cannot See), Zygote (Pid1's Family), Binder (How Apps Talk), the APK
  (Your App Is a Zip), privacy (Privacy by Design) and what Android does
  with an app you leave (Life and Death of an App).
- **Side quest: Portrait of Your Phone.** Twelve read-only questions about
  your own phone, collected in a file with the command for each answer.
  Offered after Level 5, and in the menu.
- **Touch or keyboard.** Every level can be played with touch alone (tap
  the pieces of a command) or with a keyboard alone.
- **Private by design.** No permissions at all, not even the internet; no
  ads, no tracking, no account. Everything stays in the app's own room.
- Android 10 or newer.

Tested on Android 10 and 17 emulators (phone and tablet, touch and
keyboard) and on real devices with Android 13, 16 and 17.
